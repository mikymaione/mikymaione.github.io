Open-source projects

Software

I developed the following software starting from personal needs:

  • RationesCurare, a software for the management of the personal economy;
  • MAME CGP, a cloud gaming platform for arcade games based on MAME.

Games

The following games were created for fun or as a project:

  • SaveTheZoo, a free game for Android devices, the aim of the game is to save all the animals in danger;
  • Hypogeum, it’s an open-source, online, multiplayer, game that we developed for the exam of Online Game Design;
  • Be strong, be the last, it’s an open-source, multiplayer, game that we developed for the exam of Videogame Design & Programming;
  • Gobbus, it’s a game that I developed when I was 15, there are 16 playable levels;
  • LauraRun, a platformer developed as a 22nd birthday present for my best friend Laura;
  • Dr. Dino E I Serpenti Malvagi, a frantic arcade shooter where "Dr. Dino" must defend himself against waves of evil snakes. Developed as a 23rd birthday gift for my best friend Laura, it combines immediate action with retro-inspired level design;
  • Dr. Dino Color Smash, his color-matching puzzle was developed as a 24th birthday present for my best friend Laura. Players click on colors to absorb adjacent tiles in a race to clear the board;
  • Neko ni koban, it was created during the Game Jam 2019 at Milan;
  • Senmpo, a fast-paced platformer developed in 2018 featuring an explorer arriving from deep space;
  • Lucy, a "one-tap" flying game where the protagonist, Lucy, must maneuver her broomstick through the sky while dodging obstacles. Developed as a tribute for my friend Lucia from Milan.
